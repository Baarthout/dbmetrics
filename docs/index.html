How the CRM schema is organized

The CRM platform is centered around customer data, revenue activities, and post-sale support. The relational database is organized into themed domains so that operational teams can work from a single source of truth while analytics teams can model trustworthy metrics.

8 Core tables Accounts, contacts, opportunities, and more.
3 Supporting dimensions Stages, product catalog, and success plans.
4 Activity logs Tasks, meetings, emails, and support interactions.

Domain blueprint

Customer Management

Account, contact, and address tables capture the people and organizations your teams work with. These tables provide the foundation for all downstream relationships.

  • accounts – legal entity or company.
  • contacts – individual people tied to accounts.
  • account_addresses – shipping, billing, and HQ.

Revenue Lifecycle

Opportunities, quotes, and orders document pipeline health and conversion. These tables fuel dashboards for ARR, pipeline velocity, and sales productivity.

  • opportunities – sales-qualified deals.
  • order_headers & order_lines – contract bookings and products.
  • forecasts – weekly rollups used by leadership.

Engagement Tracking

Meetings, calls, tasks, and emails provide the interaction history that helps teams prioritize outreach and measure engagement quality.

  • activities – unified log of tasks and meetings.
  • activity_outcomes – normalized results & notes.
  • campaign_responses – marketing-sourced touches.

Customer Success

Support tickets and health scores spotlight churn risk and enable proactive interventions throughout the customer lifecycle.

  • support_cases – inbound issues and requests.
  • success_plans – onboarding and adoption tasks.
  • health_snapshots – time-series health scoring.
